### CoBank milk-component report — August 27

- Registered citation sourceId: `feedstuffs`; dairy/bovine-genetics, US/international; news of a new analytical report, medium confidence with issuer attribution.
- Citation: https://www.feedstuffs.com/market-news/butterfat-boom-still-constraining-milk-protein-growth
- Primary issuer: https://www.cobank.com/knowledge-exchange/the-us-butterfat-boom-has-become-a-double-edged-sword (not independently registered).
- Both full pages explicitly dated August 27, 2026. - Useful relevance: breeding objectives and processor component needs may diverge; changes take years. Avoid implying immediate performance improvements or Proterra-specific effects.
